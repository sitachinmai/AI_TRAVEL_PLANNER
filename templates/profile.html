❓ Help & Support Center

How do I generate a multi-day itinerary?

Navigate to the Dashboard or Plan My Trip page, select any of the 195 world countries or cities, and click 'Plan Trip' or ask the AI Travel Assistant!

Can I modify specific days of a generated trip?

Yes! Ask the AI Chatbot questions like 'Make Day 2 cheaper' or 'Add more nature places' to update specific days instantly.

Where can I find destination emergency contacts?

Open any destination detail page and click the '🚨 Emergency Info' tab for verified police, medical, and tourist helpline numbers.
